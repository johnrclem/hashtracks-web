## Why not ship anyway
A single-block adapter on this page would post #2333 (20 Sep) and nothing newer. With
`upcomingOnly: true` it would be harmless, but useless: no upcoming runs ever. Without `upcomingOnly` it
would be wrong. The handoff's own fail-loud zero guard wouldn't fire either, because the block still
parses. So the source would look healthy while showing a two-month-old run.

## What would unblock it
1. **The kennel updates `hareline.htm` again.** The handoff's `AthensHashAdapter` plan then works as
   written; re-run the live check first.
2. **Or a two-source shape that doesn't depend on that page:**
   - a `STATIC_SCHEDULE` for forward runs: `FREQ=WEEKLY;BYDAY=SU`, `startTime: "11:00"`, seasonal
     `BYMONTH` that excludes the Jun–Aug summer break.
   - an HTML_SCRAPER over the `/Photos/` autoindex, a lagging record of run # + date + venue
     (`R<run>_<YYMMDD>_<Venue>`, plus the `ph<YYMMDD>_R<run>_…` variant).
   - The same autoindex is the handoff's ~915-run history backfill (R1161 2000-07-15 → R2339 2026-11-01),
     so the archive work isn't wasted either way.

   Option 2 is a design decision for the owner. The schedule-only half would show Sunday runs with no
   venue or hares.
3. Seed rows, the 5 `region.ts` edits for Greece, and the `inferCountry` disambiguation test
   (`Athens, GA` → USA) are unchanged from the handoff and still valid.
